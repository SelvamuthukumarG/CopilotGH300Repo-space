# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes OctoAcme’s project management knowledge and provides a lightweight, repeatable process for planning, delivering, and improving work across cross-functional teams. The goal is to make project execution more consistent and transparent so teammates can quickly find the right guidance, understand the expected workflow, and stay aligned on priorities, risks, and outcomes.

OctoAcme’s process follows a clear lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution and tracking, risk and communication, release and deployment, and retrospective and continuous improvement. During initiation, the team validates the business need, aligns stakeholders, and defines success criteria. Planning turns that into a prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, and an agreed definition of done. Once work begins, execution relies on daily standups, sprint reviews, clear ownership, and quality checks to keep delivery moving while tracking blockers and progress.

The project process also depends on clearly defined roles and strong communication rhythms. Project Managers coordinate schedules, dependencies, and status reporting, while Product Managers prioritize customer value and success metrics. Developers and QA partners translate that direction into shippable increments through design reviews, coding standards, testing, and acceptance validation. Communication is intentionally regular and structured, with weekly updates, stakeholder check-ins, and escalation paths for risks or incidents so decisions reach the right people at the right time.

Quality is integrated into every phase through testing, review, CI validation, release readiness checks, and post-delivery reflection. The team tracks metrics such as velocity, burndown, errors, latency, and usage to guide decisions and improve outcomes over time. At the end of a milestone or release, the retrospective captures lessons learned and action items so the process keeps maturing instead of staying static. This combination of lifecycle clarity, role ownership, transparent communication, and continuous improvement is the foundation of OctoAcme’s project management approach.
